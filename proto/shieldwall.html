Rank 1 is the front — where the two lines meet. When somebody falls, the line closes up, and whoever was behind them is suddenly in front. That is the whole trap: a spearman shoved to the front has nothing to do there.

Resolve breaks when friends fall around you. A broken fighter either wavers and gives ground, or goes wood — striking harder, shielding nothing.
